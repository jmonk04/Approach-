Importing into

For a laptop, or a real export. CSV, OFX, QFX or QIF — the column layout is read from the file, so any bank's export works without special handling. Unbudgeted

Costs that are nobody’s plan

Real money that left the account and was never budgeted for — a repair on a rental property, a one-off you could not have planned. It counts against your cash and your save rate, it counts against no limit, and it stays out of the averages that set next month’s plan, so one bad month does not reshape the next three.
